Game 1 of the 2026 World Series will mark the first time that a World Series game will take place at [[American Family Field]]. The last time a World Series game took place in Milwaukee was at [[Milwaukee County Stadium]] in [[1982 World Series#Game 5|Game 5 of its 1982 edition]]. The [[National Basketball Association]]'s [[Milwaukee Bucks]] moved the time of their home opener to 3&nbsp;p.m. CT in order to not conflict with Game 1.<ref>{{Cite web |date=October 16, 2026 |title=Bucks adjust home opener to avoid World Series time conflict |url=https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50196749/bucks-adjust-home-opener-avoid-world-series-conflict |access-date=October 18, 2026 |website=espn.com}}</ref> The Brewers named [[Jacob Misiorowski]] as their starter for Game 1, with the Rays countering with veteran [[Nick Martinez (baseball)|Nick Martinez]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Berry |first=Adam |title=Rays tab Martinez over Rasmussen in Game 1 |url=https://www.mlb.com/news/rays-name-nick-martinez-world-series-game-1-starter |access-date=2026-10-22 |website=MLB.com}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | last=Axisa | first=Mike | title=Jacob Misiorowski gets World Series Game 1 start for Brewers as he looks to reclaim his ace stuff | publisher=CBS Sports | date=2026-10-21 | url=https://www.cbssports.com/mlb/news/world-series-game-1-jacob-misiorowski-brewers-start/ | access-date=2026-10-22}}</ref> [[Robin Yount]] and [[Bud Selig]] will throw out the [[ceremonial first pitch]].<ref>{{cite web | title=Robin Yount, Bud Selig to throw out ceremonial first pitch at World Series Game 1 | website=CBS58 | date=2026-10-20 | url=https://www.cbs58.com/news/robin-yount-bud-selig-to-throw-out-ceremonial-first-pitch-at-world-series-game-1 | access-date=2026-10-22}}</ref>

* The series only ends when one team claims four wins. If neither team gets four wins after Game 7 ends, extra games will be held until the winning criteria is met.
** In such case, the team hosting Game 7 will host Game 8 in the next day. If Game 9 is necessary, then it will be held two days after Game 8, with the other team hosting.
* For the first seven games, maximum length for each game is 12 innings, thus the game may end in a tie. Starting from Game 8, there will be no length restrictions.
* For Game 3, 4, and 5, there will be [[designated hitters]], while for Game 1, 2, 6 and 7, the [[pitchers]] have to hit.
