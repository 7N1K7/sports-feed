The '''Constellation Cup''' is an international [[netball]] competition contested by [[Australia national netball team|Australia]] and [[New Zealand national netball team|New Zealand]]. The competition features a series of [[Test match (netball)|test matches]]. The two teams have competed for the trophy since [[2010 Constellation Cup|2010]].
