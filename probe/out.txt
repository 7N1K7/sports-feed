##### 2027 schedule full
==Schedule==
The 2027 schedule was fully unveiled on August 26, 2026, and consists of 32 ovals, 3 road courses, and 1 street course, as well as 3 non-championship races to be held on ovals.<ref name="2027schedule" />

'''Notes:''' Race names and title sponsors are subject to change. Not all title sponsors/names of races have been announced for 2027. For the races where a 2027 name and title sponsor has yet to be announced, the title sponsors/names of those races in 2026 are listed.

'''Bolded''' races indicate an event generally known as a [[Crown Jewel (NASCAR)|Crown Jewel race]].
