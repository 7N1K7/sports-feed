This was the third postseason meeting between the Astros and White Sox. Their previous meetings were the [[2005 World Series]] (when the Astros were members of the National League) and the ALDS in [[2021 American League Division Series#Houston vs. Chicago|2021]], with the White Sox sweeping the former, and the Astros winning the latter before falling in the World Series. The White Sox swept the Astros to advance to the ALDS for the first time since 2021.

An RBI double from [[Sam Antonacci]] and a solo homer from [[Colson Montgomery]] helped secure Game 1 for the [[Chicago White Sox|White Sox]]. An RBI single by [[Kyle Teel]] got the scoring started early for Chicago as they led for the entirety of Game 2, completing the sweep and handing the [[Houston Astros|Astros]] their sixth straight playoff game loss dating back to the [[2023 American League Championship Series|2023 ALCS]].
