===Schedule changes===
[[Homestead–Miami Speedway]] returned to its traditional date as the season finale for all three major series.<ref>{{cite web |date=May 7, 2025 |title=NASCAR Championship Weekend to return to Homestead in 2026 |url=https://www.jayski.com/2025/05/07/nascar-championship-weekend-to-return-to-homestead-in-2026 |access-date=July 25, 2025 |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media}}</ref> To accommodate this change, [[New Hampshire Motor Speedway]] was removed from The Chase.<ref name="2026schedule"/> [[Chicago Street Course]] officials announced that the [[Grant Park 165]] will not return in 2026, with hopes to revive the event in future seasons.<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Crandall |first=Kelly |date=July 18, 2025 |title=Chicago street circuit dropped from NASCAR schedule for 2026 |url=https://racer.com/2025/07/18/chicago-dropped-from-nascar-schedule-for-2026 |access-date=July 18, 2025 |magazine=[[Racer (magazine)|Racer]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250901132733/https://racer.com/2025/07/18/chicago-dropped-from-nascar-schedule-for-2026 |archive-date=September 1, 2025 |url-status=dead}}</ref> The Cup Series returned to Southern California with a [[Coronado Street Course|street race]] at [[Naval Base Coronado]] near [[San Diego]].<ref>{{cite web |date=July 23, 2025 |title=San Diego to host NASCAR's latest cup series innovation with a street race at Naval Base Coronado |url=https://www.jayski.com/2025/07/23/nascar-near-a-deal-to-run-san-diego-street-course-race/ |access-date=July 23, 2025 |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media}}</ref> To accommodate this change, [[NASCAR Cup Series at Mexico City|Mexico City]] was dropped from the schedule due to a conflict with the [[2026 FIFA World Cup]].<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/6523588/2025/07/30/nascar-2026-schedule-mexico-city-chicagoland/ |title=Mexico City is out for NASCAR's 2026 schedule; Chicagoland likely in: Sources |website=[[The Athletic]] |publisher=[[New York Times]] |last=Bianchi |first=Jordan |date=July 30, 2025 |access-date=July 30, 2025}}</ref> The [[NASCAR All-Star Race]] moved from [[North Wilkesboro Speedway]] to [[Dover Motor Speedway]], giving the former a [[NASCAR Cup Series at North Wilkesboro Speedway|points race]], the first regular-season race held since [[1996 NASCAR Winston Cup Series|1996]].<ref name="2026schedule"/> [[Chicagoland Speedway]] returned to the schedule for the first time since [[2019 Monster Energy NASCAR Cup Series|2019]].<ref name="2026schedule"/> The [[Charlotte Motor Speedway]] [[Bank of America 400|fall race]] moved away from the [[Bank of America Roval 400|roval layout]] to the oval after eight years as a road course race.<ref name="Gluck"/>

==Season summary==
===Preseason races===
Due to [[January 30 – February 2, 2026 nor'easter|a historic snowstorm]] over the United States, the [[2026 Cook Out Clash|Cook Out Clash]] was postponed three times from its original Saturday and Sunday dates, before ultimately being condensed to a one-day event on Wednesday, February 4.<ref>{{cite web |date=January 29, 2026 |title=Cook Out Clash Saturday events postponed at Bowman Gray Stadium |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/01/29/cook-out-clash-saturday-events-postponed-at-bowman-gray-stadium/ |website=[[NASCAR]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=January 31, 2025 |title=Clash postponed to Monday |url=https://www.jayski.com/2026/01/31/cook-out-clash-postponed-to-monday/}}</ref><ref name="clashpostponed" /> Reigning champion [[Kyle Larson]] won pole position. Weather plagued the event, causing NASCAR to mandate use of wet weather tires after a scheduled half-race break. After an event record 17 cautions, [[Ryan Preece]] won the Clash, joining [[Jeff Gordon]] and [[Denny Hamlin]] as drivers who won the Clash before winning their first points paying race.<ref>{{cite web |date=February 4, 2026 |title=Ryan Preece wins Cook Out Clash at Bowman Gray amid winter weather |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/02/04/cook-out-clash-recap-2026-ryan-preece-wins-bowman-gray/ |website=[[NASCAR]]}}</ref>

The [[2026 America 250 Florida Duel at Daytona|Duel at Daytona]] races set the starting order for the [[2026 Daytona 500|Daytona 500]], with the exception of the front row, which is set by the fastest two times in qualifying. [[Kyle Busch]] and [[Chase Briscoe]] won the pole and second place respectively, and therefore started first in each duel race.<ref>{{Cite web |author=Staff Report |date=February 13, 2025 |title=Busch wins pole for Daytona 500; Briscoe on front row for second straight year |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/02/11/kyle-busch-wins-pole-for-daytona-500-briscoe-on-front-row-for-second-straight-year/ |access-date=February 12, 2026 |website=[[NASCAR]]}}</ref> During the first Duel, [[RFK Racing]] (whose cars used the number font of late RFK driver [[Greg Biffle]], as did [[Kaulig Racing]]'s no. 16) dominated the majority of the race, seeking to help [[Corey LaJoie]] clinch among the "Open" cars, as he had not qualified for the race. Unfortunately for LaJoie, a last lap wreck ended his chances of making the 500. [[Casey Mears]], despite a spin earlier in the race, dodged LaJoie during the wreck, clinching his spot into the race, making his first Daytona 500 start since [[2019 Daytona 500|2019]].<ref name="Daytona Duels Recap">{{cite web |title=Logano, Elliott win Duel races; Mears, Alfredo lock into Daytona 500 |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/02/12/joey-logano-wins-first-duel-race-mears-locks-into-daytona-500/ |access-date=February 12, 2026 |website=[[NASCAR]]}}</ref>

During the second Duel, which ran caution-free, [[Chase Elliott]] finished 0.065 seconds ahead of [[Carson Hocevar]].<ref name="Daytona Duels Recap" /> [[Anthony Alfredo]] finished in position to make the Daytona 500 as the last open car, but was disqualified after a post-race technical inspection found that his car's cooling hoses were not secured. Due to the disqualification, the next-best finishing open car, driven by [[B. J. McLeod]], qualified for the race.<ref>{{Cite web |date=February 12, 2026 |title=Anthony Alfredo finish disallowed following second duel race |url=https://www.jayski.com/2026/02/12/anthony-alfredo-finish-disallowed-following-second-duel-race/ |access-date=February 13, 2026 |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]] |publisher=NASCAR Digital Media Network}}</ref>

===Regular season===
NASCAR moved the [[2026 Daytona 500|Daytona 500]] start time forward an hour to 1:30&nbsp;p.m. eastern time due to the threat of rain in the evening.<ref name="Daytona500movedup" /> [[Zane Smith]] won stage one after staying out in the middle of pit cycles, as well as getting his first career stage win. Stage two featured a battle between polesitter [[Kyle Busch]] and [[Kyle Larson]] for the lead for the majority of the stage.
