The '''2026 NRL season''' was the 119th season of professional [[rugby league]] in Australia and the 29th season run by the [[National Rugby League]] (NRL). The regular season was followed by a finals series contested by the top eight teams on the competition ladder. The [[Penrith Panthers]] were the minor premiers, while the [[Sydney Roosters]] defeated the [[Newcastle Knights]] in the Grand Final to win their 16th premiership.

The season recorded the highest aggregate and average regular season crowds in first-grade rugby league history, surpassing the record set the previous year.

The season was also the highest scoring in first-grade rugby league history, with 10,167 points scored across 213 games, surpassing the 253 game [[1998 NRL season|1998 season]] in which 10,131 points were scored. At an average of 47.73 points scored per match, 2026 was also the highest average scoring season since [[2004 NRL season|2004]].{{efn|in which 9,086 points were scored across 189 matches, an average of 48.07 points per game.}}
