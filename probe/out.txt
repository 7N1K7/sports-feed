######## US practice now
== Practice ==
Three [[Formula One racing#Free practice|free practice sessions]] are being held for the event. The first free practice session was held on October 23, 2026, at 12:30 local time ([[UTC–5]]), and was topped by [[Charles Leclerc]] ([[Scuderia Ferrari|Ferrari]]) ahead of [[Max Verstappen]] ([[Red Bull Racing|Red Bull]]) and [[Lando Norris]] ([[McLaren]]). The second free practice session will be held on the same day, at 16:00 local time. The third free practice session will be held on October 24, 2026, at 12:30 local time.<ref name="Cal"/>

== Qualifying ==
[[Formula One race weekend#Current format|Qualifying]] will be held on October 24, 2026, at 16:00 local time ([[UTC–5]]), and will determine the starting grid order for the race.<ref name="Cal"/>
