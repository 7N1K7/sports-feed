It was the seventeenth round of the [[2026 Formula One World Championship]] and the sixth and final Grand Prix in the season to utilise the [[Formula One racing#Sprint|sprint format]].\n\n[[Max Verstappen]] ([[Red Bull Racing|Red Bull]]) converted his sprint pole position to a victory, while championship contender [[George Russell (racing driver)|George Russell]] ([[Mercedes-Benz in Formula One|Mercedes]]) crashed out of an early lead of the sprint. Verstappen went on to achieve pole position later on in the qualifying session ahead of the two [[Scuderia Ferrari|Ferrari]] teammates [[Charles Leclerc]] and [[Lewis Hamilton]]. Verstappen won his first [[Singapore Grand Prix]] ahead of Leclerc and championship leader [[Kimi Antonelli]].\n\n==Background==\nThe event was held at the [[Marina Bay Street Circuit]] in [[Marina Bay, Singapore|Marina Bay]] for the 17th time in the circuit's history, across the weekend of 9–11 October.<ref>{{cite web |title=Singapore |url=https://www.statsf1.com/en/circuit-singapour.aspx |website=StatsF1.com |access-date=5 October 2026}}</ref> The Grand Prix was the seventeenth round of the [[2026 Formula One World Championship]] and the 17th running of the [[Singapore Grand Prix]] as a round of the Formula One World Championship. It was also the sixth and final Grand Prix in the season to utilise the [[Formula One racing#Sprint|sprint format]] and the first time that the Singapore Grand Prix featured it.<ref>{{cite web |title=Grands Prix Singapore|url=https://www.statsf1.com/en/grand-prix-singapore.aspx |website=StatsF1.com |access-date=5 October 2026}}</ref><ref name=\"sprint\">{{Cite news |title=Formula 1 and FIA announce 2026 Sprint Calendar |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/formula-1-and-fia-announce-2026-sprint-calendar.3PyLPAazrBNe8kQIS3wOfY |access-date=5 October 2026 |work=Formula 1|date=5 October 2026}}</ref>\n\n=== Championship standings before the race ===\nGoing into the weekend, [[Kimi Antonelli]] led the [[List of Formula One World Drivers' Champions|Drivers' Championship]] with 320 points, 84 points ahead of teammate [[George Russell (racing driver)|George Russell]] in second and 106 ahead of [[Lewis Hamilton]] in third. Ferme|date=10 October 2026 |access-date=10 October 2026 |publisher=[[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile]]}}</ref>\n\n== Sprint ==\nThe [[Formula One race weekend#Sprint|sprint]] was held on 10 October 2026, and was scheduled to start at 17:00 local time ([[UTC+8]]), before being delayed to 17:30 due to heavy rain affecting the track. It was reduced from 21 to 20 laps.<ref name=\"race-page\" />\n\n=== Sprint report ===\nAfter two formation laps that led to a standing start, [[George Russell (racing driver)|George Russell]] ([[Mercedes-Benz in Formula One|Mercedes]]) took the lead from polesitter [[Max Verstappen]] ([[Red Bull Racing|Red Bull]]) into the first corner. Due to damp track conditions leading to a loss of traction, on lap two the leading Russell lost control of his Mercedes on the pit straight and spun, retiring instead with his car briefly on the racing line before hitting the wall. At the same time, numerous retirements were reported: [[Sergio Pérez]] ([[Cadillac in Formula One|Cadillac]]) hit [[Lance Stroll]] ([[Aston Martin in Formula One|Aston Martin]]) at turn 16, [[Isack Hadjar]] (Red Bull) retired after dropping positions early on due to power unit failures, and [[Franco Colapinto]] ([[Alpine F1 Team|Alpine]]) retired due to a communications issue with his car. Meanwhile [[Arvid Lindblad]] ([[Racing Bulls]]) spun to last place and recovered to thirteenth by the flag.<ref name=\"F1COMSprint\">{{cite web |date=10 October 2026 |title=Verstappen wins dramatic, rain-hit Singapore Sprint as Russell crashes and McLarens collide |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-wins-dramatic-rain-hit-singapore-sprint-as-russell-crashes-and-mclarens-collide.4r90mH2skljBa5sinFAsyp |access-date=10 October 2026 |work=Formula1.com}}</ref>\n\nThe resulting safety car ended on lap 6. [[Alexander Albon]] ([[Williams Racing|Williams]]) hit [[Gabriel Bortoleto]] ([[Audi in Formula One|Audi]]), causing the Brazilian driver to retire alongside [[Valtteri Bottas]] (Cadillac). Albon himself retired soon afterward. On the final lap [[Lando Norris]] spun with [[Oscar Piastri]] (both [[McLaren]]) while battling each other. Due to this, Norris dropped to eighth and Piastri fourteenth. The incident was investigated by the stewards, who did not impose any penalties.<ref name=\"F1COMSprint\" />\n\nVerstappen won his first sprint of the season, his first at [[Marina Bay Street Circuit]], and [[Lewis Hamilton]] ([[Scuderia Ferrari|Ferrari]]) finished second ahead of teammate [[Charles Leclerc]], who inherited third. 2026, at 20:00 local time ([[UTC+8]]), and was run for 62 laps.<ref name=\"race-page\"/>\n\n=== Race report ===\nPrior to the start of the race, Red Bull's [[Isack Hadjar]] and both Aston Martin drivers [[Fernando Alonso]] and [[Lance Stroll]] were all required to start from the pit lane as all have made modifications under [[parc fermé]] conditions after Saturday's qualifying, promoting championship runner-up [[George Russell (racing driver)|George Russell]] to nineteenth on the grid instead of starting from 21st position.<ref name=\"F1COMRace\">{{cite web |date=11 October 2026 |title=Verstappen seals commanding Singapore Grand Prix victory from Leclerc and Antonelli |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-seals-commanding-singapore-grand-prix-victory-from-leclerc-and-antonelli.49wDzysiVqi5cloGjle40A |access-date=18 October 2026 |work=Formula 1}}</ref><ref name=\"AutosportRace\">{{cite web |last=Vinel |first=Ben |date=11 October 2026 |title=F1 Singapore GP: Verstappen takes comfortable victory from Leclerc and Antonelli |url=https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/f1-singapore-gp-report/10864312/ |access-date=18 October 2026 |work=[[Autosport]]}}</ref>\n\nWhen the lights went out, both Verstappen and Leclerc got off their respective grid boxes well, with the latter having a slightly better reaction time, enabling him to attack Verstappen heading into turn 1. Verstappen opted to block the attack by defending hard against the Monégasque driver, allowing him to keep the lead, whilst Hamilton was able to defend his position against Antonelli and both McLaren drivers. On lap seven, [[Gabriel Bortoleto]] rounded up a disappointing weekend by coming to a halt with a gearbox failure, leaving his Audi stranded at the pit lane exit, triggering a [[virtual safety car]]. During the VSC, no tyre changes were applied. Hamilton, who opted for the medium compound to start the race as opposed to the drivers around him, was initially able to keep up the speed compared to both Verstappen and Leclerc by setting fastest laps, but saw his advantage slip away as both front runners were able to build a substantial gap heading into the mid-portion of the race, before Leclerc then lost ground on Verstappen due to tyre disadvantage.<ref name=\"F1COMRace\" /><ref name=\"AutosportRace\" />\n\nWhile the race remained largely even due to the middle stage, Stroll caused a second virtual safety car phase by stopping his car due to a clutch issue, triggering a series of pit stops including most of the front runners. Hamilton instead opted to stay out on worn soft tyres which put him into a vulnerable position heading into the final stages of the race, as he got past by Leclerc, who tried to use his tyre advantage towards Verstappen in an attempt to close the 14-second-gap to the leader with roughly 20 laps to go as Verstappen decided to stay out as well following the second VSC, mainly to maintain the substantial gap he had built up earlier on in order to avoid himself getting leapfrogged by Leclerc.<ref name=\"F1COMRace\" /><ref name=\"AutosportRace\" />  \n\nHamilton, who saw his chances of getting a second consecutive podium slipping away due to a failed strategy, tried to defend attacks from both Norris and Antonelli on lap 54, causing him to go wide after the first turn. Championship leader Antonelli took advantage of the slight confusion, as Norris took a rather unconventional racing line into the corner complex of two and three, taking over fourth place from the reigning champion. Later at the same lap, Antonelli was able to get past Hamilton in order to take third, whilst Norris completed his overtaking attempt towards Hamilton one lap later to claim fourth. On the final lap, the seven-time world champion saw himself getting overtaken by Piastri in the end for fifth, with the former taking full responsibility for his misjudgement to stay out on old tyres, as it was later revealed that Ferrari initially planned to pit Hamilton during the second VSC phase in order to secure a podium, which he dismissed.<ref name=\"F1COMRace\" /><ref name=\"AutosportRace\" /> \n\nWith Leclerc being unable to close the gap, Verstappen crossed the line to take an unchallenged win, as he led every single lap of the race besides taking pole position on Saturday. The Dutchman was able to take his first Grand Prix victory at the streets of Singapore, his second consecutive win after winning in Sepang seven days ago and his 73rd victory overall. He was denied of his seventh career grand chelem as Leclerc took the fastest lap, with the latter taking second place ahead of Antonelli, who rounded up the podium by being on the rostrum for the fifteenth time in 17 races this season, whilst Leclerc achieved his first podium since [[2026 Belgian Grand Prix|Belgium]]. Norris and Piastri settled for fourth and fifth, respectively. Hamilton took the chequered flag in sixth, with Lawson, Hülkenberg, Russell and Hadjar completing the top ten.<ref name=\"F1COMRace\" /><ref name=\"AutosportRace\" />\n\n=== Post-race and championship impact ===\nHis second consecutive victory elevated Verstappen to fourth in the Drivers' Championship, whilst both Leclerc and Norris were demoted one position each. After surpassing the points total of the latter two, Verstappen saw himself only eight points behind third-placed Hamilton. Antonelli moved to 340 points, enabling him to extend his gap towards teammate and nearest rival Russell to 102 points.<ref name=\"Website-1\">{{Cite web |last=Juilhard |first=Cyprien |date=11 October |website=Auto Hebdo}}</ref>\n\nIn the Constructors' Championship, Mercedes remained in the leading position, but Ferrari was able to narrow the gap to 134 points with six races to go, whilst Red Bull closed the points disadvantage to reigning champions McLaren to just seven.<ref name=\"Website-1\" />\n\n=== Race classification ===\n{| class=\"wikitable sortable\" style=\"font-size: 85%;\"\n! It will be the eighteenth round of the [[2026 Formula One World Championship]].\n\n==Background==\nThe event will be held at the [[Circuit of the Americas]] in [[Austin, Texas]], for the 14th time in the circuit's history, across the weekend of October 23–25, 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Austin |url=https://www.statsf1.com/en/circuit-austin.aspx |access-date=October 19, 2026 |website=StatsF1}}</ref> The Grand Prix will be the eighteenth round of the [[2026 Formula One World Championship]] and the 47th running of the [[United States Grand Prix]] as a round of the Formula One World Championship.<ref>{{cite web |title=Grands Prix USA |url=https://www.statsf1.com/en/grand-prix-usa.aspx |access-date=October 19, 2026 |website=StatsF1}}</ref>\n\n=== Championship standings before the race ===\nGoing into the weekend, [[Kimi Antonelli]] leads the [[List of Formula One World Drivers' Champions|Drivers' Championship]] with 340 points, 102 points ahead of teammate [[George Russell (racing driver)|George Russell]] in second and 111 ahead of [[Lewis Hamilton]] in third. [[Mercedes-Benz in Formula One|Mercedes]], with 578 points, leads the [[World Constructors' Championship|Constructors' Championship]] from [[Scuderia Ferrari|Ferrari]] and [[McLaren]], who are second and third with 444 and 339 points, respectively.<ref>{{cite web |date=October 11, 2026 |title=Singapore 2026 – Championship |url=https://www.statsf1.com/en/2026/singapour/championnat.aspx |access-date=October 19, 2026 |website=StatsF1}}</ref>\n\n==== Championship permutations ====\nGoing into the race, there are 150 points left through six remaining rounds, with six drivers in mathematical contention: [[Kimi Antonelli]], [[George Russell (racing driver)|George Russell]] (both [[Mercedes-Benz in Formula One|Mercedes]]), [[Lewis Hamilton]] ([[Scuderia Ferrari|Ferrari]]), [[Max Verstappen]] ([[Red Bull Racing|Red Bull]]), [[Charles Leclerc]] (Ferrari), and defending Drivers' Champion [[Lando Norris]] ([[McLaren]]). Leclerc and Norris trail distantly and can be eliminated from contention by a variety of scenarios.<ref name=\"F1Champ\">{{cite web |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/points-permutations-when-is-the-earliest-antonelli-could-win-the-world-championship.24UKT2fsQl4GmC5DMrmYUy |title=Points permutations: When is the earliest Antonelli could win the World Championship? |date=October 13, 2026 |access-date=October 16, 2026 |website=Formula1.com }}</ref>\n\nAntonelli has an opportunity to secure his maiden Drivers' Championship by winning the race.<ref name=\"F1Champ\"/><ref name=\"ASChamp\"/> If his teammate Russell finishes ninth or lower, he is eliminated from contention. Meanwhile, Hamilton and Verstappen both have outside chances to remain in contention should they place highly at this event. To remain in contention, Hamilton must not be outscored by fourteen points, and Verstappen must not be outscored by six.<ref name=\"ASChamp\">{{cite web |url=https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/how-can-antonelli-become-f1-world-champion-at-us-gp/10864377/ |title=How can Antonelli become F1 world champion at US GP? |last=Hardy |first=Ed |date=October 11, 2026 |website=Autosport}}</ref> However, Antonelli could also secure the Drivers' Championship at this event should the season-ending [[2026 Qatar Grand Prix|Qatar]] and [[2026 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix|Abu Dhabi]] Grands Prix be canceled.{{efn|The [[Middle East]] region, where the [[Qatar Grand Prix|Qatar]] and [[Abu Dhabi Grand Prix|Abu Dhabi]] Grands Prix are held, saw sustained conflicts occur due to the [[2026 Iran war]] and concurrent [[Yemeni civil war (2014–present)|Yemeni civil war]], with attacks occurring in Grand Prix host countries. These conflicts led to the [[2026 Saudi Arabian Grand Prix|Saudi Arabian Grand Prix]] being canceled and the [[2026 Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia|Bahrain Grand Prix]] being moved to [[Sepang International Circuit]] in [[Sepang]], [[Malaysia]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Benson |first=Andrew |date=March 14, 2026 |title=F1 cancels Bahrain & Saudi Arabia Grands Prix because of Iran war impact |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/c5ykne3g305o |access-date=March 15, 2026 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Formula 1 and FIA confirm Malaysia will join 2026 calendar as host venue for Bahrain Grand Prix |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/formula-1-and-fia-confirm-malaysia-will-join-2026-calendar-as-host-venue-for-bahrain-grand-prix.6lL7vjFEM2VVynRHvg1TCf |website=Formula1.com |publisher=Formula One World Championship Limited |date=July 26, 2026 |access-date=July 26, 2026}}</ref>}} Should this occur, 125 points would be available over five remaining rounds. He could secure the title by simply winning this Grand Prix, as there would not be enough points for his closest competitors to challenge him.<ref name=\"NewChampionPermutations\">{{cite web |url=https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/formula-1-prepares-to-trigger-imola-season-finale-amid-escalating-regional-security-threats/10864677/ |title=Kimi Antonelli’s title hopes boosted by proposed F1 calendar overhaul |website=Motorsport |last=Mazumder |first=Rahaan |date=October 12, 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/imola-or-qatar-and-abu-dhabi-whats-really-going-on-with-the-end-of-the-2026-f1-season/10858624/ |title=Imola or Qatar and Abu Dhabi? [[Mercedes-Benz in Formula One|Mercedes]], the current World Constructors' Championship leaders.\n}}\nThe '''2026 FIA Formula One World Championship''' is a motor racing championship for [[Formula One cars]] and the 77th running of the [[Formula One World Championship]]. It is recognised by the [[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile]] (FIA), the governing body of international [[motorsport]], as the highest class of competition for [[open-wheel racing car]]s. The championship is contested over twenty-three Grands Prix held around the world. It began in March and is scheduled to end in December. Drivers and teams compete for the titles of [[World Drivers' Champion]] and [[World Constructors' Champion]], respectively. [[Lando Norris]] is the reigning World Drivers' Champion, while [[McLaren]]-[[Mercedes AMG High Performance Powertrains|Mercedes]] are the reigning World Constructors' Champions.\n\nThe 2026 season features a major set of regulation changes with a revised power unit configuration and new active aerodynamics along with the addition of an eleventh team competing for the first time since {{F1|2016}}. [[Audi in Formula One|Audi]], who acquired [[Sauber Motorsport|Sauber]] in {{F1|2024}}, enter as a [[factory-backed|works team]] with its own power unit, while [[Cadillac in Formula One|Cadillac]] are making their debut in the championship using [[Scuderia Ferrari|Ferrari]] power units. [[Honda in Formula One|Honda]], through its [[Honda Racing Corporation]] subsidiary, entered into an exclusive works team agreement with [[Aston Martin in Formula One|Aston Martin]], and are supplying them with their own power unit after ending its relationship with [[Red Bull Racing]]. [[Ford in Formula One|Ford]] returned to the sport for the first time since {{F1|2004}}, supporting [[Red Bull Powertrains]] in supplying power units to Red Bull Racing and [[Racing Bulls]]. [[Renault in Formula One|Renault]] is no longer an engine supplier as [[Alpine F1 Team|Alpine]] switched to Mercedes power units.\n\nThe [[2026 Iran war]] affected the championship's schedule. The [[2026 Saudi Arabian Grand Prix|Saudi Arabian Grand Prix]] was cancelled, and the [[Bahrain Grand Prix]] was postponed and ultimately [[2026 Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia|hosted in Malaysia]], with the return of Formula One to [[Sepang International Circuit]].\n\n== Entries ==\nEach team is required to enter at least two drivers, one for each of the two mandatory cars.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss02_-_2024-12-11.pdf|title=2026 Formula One Sporting Regulations – Issue 2|publisher=[[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile]]|date=11 December 2024|access-date=14 December 2024|archive-date=14 December 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241214150533/https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_b_sporting_-_iss02_-_2024-12-11.pdf|url-status=live}}</ref> All teams compete with tyres supplied by [[Pirelli]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Pirelli to continue as Formula 1's exclusive tyre supplier until 2027 |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/pirelli-to-continue-as-formula-1s-exclusive-tyre-supplier-until-2027.7xJIxJyMe84N3p7k4iIMjK |date=10 October 2023 |work=Formula 1 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240330165155/https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/pirelli-to-continue-as-formula-1s-exclusive-tyre-supplier-until-2027.7xJIxJyMe84N3p7k4iIMjK |archive-date=30 March 2024 |access-date=30 April 2024}}</ref>\n\n<div style=\"overflow-x: auto;\">\n{|class=\"wikitable sortable\" style=\"font-size:85%;\"\n|+{{nowrap|Teams and drivers that are contracted to compete in the 2026 World Championship}}\n!scope=\"col\" rowspan=\"2\"|Entrant\n!scope=\"col\" rowspan=\"2\"|[[List of Formula One constructors|Constructor]]\n! The term \"boost mode\" is used to describe the normal deployment of the energy recovery system (ERS) to defend or attack; \"active aerodynamics\" (or simply \"active aero\") to describe the moveable front and rear wing elements – in both higher and lower drag/downforce configuration (called \"corner mode\" and \"straightline mode\" respectively); \"recharge\" to describe when a car is harvesting electrical energy; and \"overtake mode\" to describe a mode where extra energy stored is used in order to help one driver overtake another, but only if the driver behind is within one second of the one ahead (similar to pre-2026 DRS deployment rules).<ref>{{cite news |last1=Noble |first1=Jon |title=New F1 2026 mode names and car images revealed |url=https://www.the-race.com/all/new-f1-2026-mode-names-and-car-images-revealed/ |access-date=17 December 2025 |work=The Race |date=17 December 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Explained: The new key terms for F1's new-for-2026 rules |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/explained-the-new-key-terms-for-formula-1s-new-for-2026-rules.3T5BU6TC9quGcIpGzoWkY0 |website=Formula One |access-date=17 December 2025}}</ref>\n\nDue to [[Circuit de Monaco|the track]]'s tight, twisty nature, the only Grand Prix where active aero was disabled was the [[2026 Monaco Grand Prix|Monaco Grand Prix]], which historically had one DRS zone.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Seymour |first=Mike |date=4 June 2026 |title=Explained: Why Active Aero will not be used at the Monaco Grand Prix |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/explained-why-active-aero-will-not-be-used-at-the-monaco-grand-prix.4nLtpjM9ZTUbPBQLmeAbj4 |access-date=7 June 2026 |website=Formula 1}}</ref>\n\n====Safety features====\nThe regulations for the front impact structure (FIS) were updated with the intent to enhance [[Safety in Formula One|safety]] during crashes. A two-stage FIS design has been introduced to address previous issues where the structure detached near the survival cell after a primary collision, leaving the vehicle vulnerable to further impacts. Side intrusion protection, particularly around the cockpit and fuel cell was also improved. These upgrades aim to shield critical areas of the car during side collisions, while maintaining the vehicle's weight. The roll hoop's strength was improved, withstanding loads increased from 16 ''g'' to 20 ''g'', aligning with safety standards of other [[single-seater]] series. The load testing requirements were raised from 141&nbsp;kN to 167&nbsp;kN.<ref name=\"Safety\">{{cite news|last=Barretto|first=Lawrence|url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/explained-2026-aerodynamic-regulations-fia-x-mode-z-mode-.26c1CtOzCmN3GfLMywrgb2|title=From more agile cars to 'X-mode' and 'Z-mode' – unpicking the 2026 aerodynamics regulations|work=Formula 1|date=6 June 2024|access-date=20 December 2024|archive-date=1 December 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241201104501/https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/explained-2026-aerodynamic-regulations-fia-x-mode-z-mode-.26c1CtOzCmN3GfLMywrgb2|url-status=live}}</ref> To further safeguard drivers and [[track marshals]], lateral safety lights were mandated. Antonelli rounded out the podium, finishing 1.9 seconds behind Russell and three tenths behind Verstappen. During the race, multiple duels between both Ferraris was observed, Sainz retired on the pit straight after an electrical problem, and the Cadillacs retired early following overheating brake issues.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/russell-seals-victory-in-thrilling-austrian-grand-prix-ahead-of-verstappen-and-antonelli.5kjw8LmvWRcsaFgjCr8aWw |title=Russell seals victory in thrilling Austrian Grand Prix ahead of Verstappen and Antonelli |website=Formula1.com |date=28 June 2026 |access-date=28 June 2026}}</ref>\n\nAt the [[2026 British Grand Prix|British Grand Prix]], Hamilton took sprint pole position, though Antonelli passed him for the win. Antonelli built on this momentum into Grand Prix qualifying and took pole position, however he received wheelspin at the start and dropped behind the [[Scuderia Ferrari|Ferrari]] cars of Leclerc and Hamilton. Leclerc led a majority of the race; during the closing stages, Antonelli, who was closing in on Leclerc, damaged the front-left wheel shield after running over a kerb. This hampered the car's ability to turn and he had to pit twice to fix the issue, before then attaining a five-second penalty for repeated track limits infringements. The race ended under safety car after Verstappen, who was running third, had to retire after a rear wing failure resulted in him spinning off into the gravel trap at Stowe. Leclerc won the race, his first British Grand Prix victory and first victory since the [[2024 United States Grand Prix]]. Russell and Hamilton completed the podium at their home race with both drivers recovering from earlier setbacks; Russell from a slow puncture and Hamilton from a five-second penalty for a false start. Antonelli finished outside the points in fifteenth after the track limits penalty was added on to his race time. In addition to Verstappen, [[Alex Albon]] and [[Nico Hülkenberg]] also retired.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/leclerc-wins-dramatic-british-grand-prix-from-russell-and-hamilton-as-antonelli-suffers-issue.3bGl6laB38GIr1PfxJTNmY |title=Leclerc wins dramatic British Grand Rrix from Russell and Hamilton as Antonelli suffers issue |website=Formula1.com |date=5 July 2026 |access-date=20 July 2026}}</ref>\n\nAntonelli started the [[2026 Belgian Grand Prix|Belgian Grand Prix]] from pole position, although Verstappen briefly overtook him on the opening lap before the Mercedes driver reclaimed the lead along the Kemmel Straight. A virtual safety car brought out for gravel on the racing line allowed Leclerc to jump both Antonelli and Verstappen through the pit stops and take the lead, but Antonelli closed the gap and passed Leclerc on lap 34. He held off the Ferrari driver to claim his sixth victory of the season, with Verstappen completing the podium. Hamilton recovered from his penalty to finish fourth ahead of Piastri, while [[Isack Hadjar]] progressed from twenty-first on the grid to sixth. The result extended Antonelli's championship lead to 45 points over Hamilton and 50 over Russell.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-beats-leclerc-to-belgian-grand-prix-win-to-extend-title-lead-as-russell-retires.MAqSJSgXvRP6dLbwiRbWI |title=Antonelli beats Leclerc to Belgian Grand Prix win to extend title lead as Russell retires |website=Formula1.com |date=19 July 2026 |access-date=27 July 2026}}</ref>\n\nThe [[2026 Hungarian Grand Prix|Hungarian Grand Prix]] saw [[Aston Martin in Formula One|Aston Martin]] bring no less than 16 updates to their car, with [[Fernando Alonso]] giving the team their first Q2 appearance of the season as a result. Norris edged out Hamilton by 0.012 seconds to take the seventeenth pole of his career, and become the first non-[[Mercedes-Benz in Formula One|Mercedes]] driver to take pole in 2026. Both Hamilton and Antonelli would receive three-place grid penalties post-qualifying; Hamilton for impeding [[Oscar Piastri]], and Antonelli for failing to slow sufficiently for yellow flags. Piastri moved into the lead on the opening lap after Norris ran wide at turn 2, and proceeded to lead the first half of the race. After Piastri's second stop, he was slowed by minor contact with the lapped car of [[Carlos Sainz Jr.]] and further delayed by other lapped cars, allowing Norris to extend his second stint and emerge ahead of Piastri after pitting on lap 40. Norris controlled the remainder of the race to claim his and [[McLaren]]'s first victory of the season, while Piastri retired from second place with a gearbox failure, triggering a virtual safety car with 14 laps remaining. Ferrari pitted Hamilton under the virtual safety car, dropping him behind Verstappen and Antonelli, before a five-second penalty for speeding in the pit lane demoted him to fifth behind Leclerc. Verstappen and Antonelli completed the podium, with Hadjar finishing sixth and Russell recovering from stalling at the start to take seventh. [[Nico Hülkenberg]] finished ninth, scoring his first points of the season and first as an [[Audi in Formula One|Audi]] driver. Antonelli extended his championship lead to 50 points over Hamilton and 59 over Russell, with Leclerc and Norris 81 and 91 points behind respectively heading into the summer break.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/norris-beats-verstappen-and-antonelli-to-victory-in-dramatic-hungarian-gp.5JQmohRAKABD2asRsMWogj |title=Norris beats Verstappen and Antonelli to victory in dramatic Hungarian GP |website=Formula1.com |date=26 July 2026 |access-date=27 July 2026}}</ref>\n\nFollowing the summer break, the season resumed with the [[2026 Dutch Grand Prix|Dutch Grand Prix]]; it was the last to be hosted at [[Circuit Zandvoort]], and the first and only to be a sprint event. [[Red Bull Racing|Red Bull]] driver [[Isack Hadjar]] missed the event after sustaining a wrist injury during the summer break; [[Liam Lawson]] was promoted from [[Racing Bulls]] to replace him, and reserve driver [[Yuki Tsunoda]] was called up to drive for Racing Bulls. Russell won the sprint with a lights-to-flag drive from pole, ahead of Leclerc and Norris. Norris responded in qualifying for the Grand Prix, taking pole position by a tenth over Russell. The Grand Prix was interrupted by a lengthy red flag following a heavy crash for Verstappen at turn 14 on the opening lap, the Dutchman emerged unharmed but had to retire from his home race. Antonelli took the lead at the ensuing [[standing start]] when the race resumed; he led for a majority of the race, closely shadowed by Norris. On lap 54, Norris took advantage of Antonelli getting held up by Hamilton who was on an alternate strategy to pass him into turn 1, he then passed Hamilton later on the same lap and moved into the lead. Norris survived two further virtual safety car periods to take his second win in a row, with Antonelli finishing second and extending his championship lead to 59 points. Russell took the final spot on the podium after holding off the Ferraris of Hamilton and Leclerc, who finished fourth and fifth respectively.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/norris-wins-dramatic-dutch-grand-prix-from-antonelli-and-russell-as-verstappen-crashes-out.Zn7iYevVGp5eHzFkTEAz7 |title=Norris wins dramatic Dutch Grand Prix from Antonelli and Russell as Verstappen crashes out |website=Formula1.com |date=23 August 2026 |access-date=23 August 2026}}</ref>\n\n[[Alpine F1 Team|Alpine]] driver [[Pierre Gasly]] claimed his first career pole position in qualifying for the [[2026 Italian Grand Prix|Italian Grand Prix]] ahead of Russell and [[Oscar Piastri]], although Piastri received a three-place grid penalty for impeding [[Liam Lawson]]. Leclerc crashed heavily at the Parabolica on the second lap of the Grand Prix, bringing out the red flag; he emerged unharmed but his car was completely destroyed. On the subsequent restart, Russell, who had taken the lead from Gasly before the red flag, led away but was soon hounded by Verstappen. Antonelli, who started down the order after taking a grid penalty for exceeding his quota of power unit elements, worked his way through the field and caught up to the leading pair. During a virtual safety car period caused by [[Lance Stroll]] stopping on track with a hydraulic failure, Russell chose to stay out while Antonelli and Verstappen pitted for fresh tyres. Antonelli, making use of the substantial tyre offset, caught and passed his teammate in the closing stages of the race to win from nineteenth on the grid. Following heavy rain and two delays to the start, Verstappen recovered from losing the lead at the start to take his first victory of the season, ahead of Antonelli and Hamilton. (officially known as the '''Formula 1 MSC Cruises Grande Prêmio de São Paulo 2025''') was a [[Formula One]] motor race held on 9 November 2025 at the [[Interlagos Circuit]] in [[São Paulo]], Brazil. It was the twenty-first round of the [[2025 Formula One World Championship]] and the fifth of six Grands Prix in the season to utilise the [[Formula One racing#Sprint|sprint format]]. [[Lando Norris]] of [[McLaren]] took pole position for the [[Formula One racing#Sprint|sprint]] and main race, converting them both to wins. In the sprint, the podium was completed by [[Mercedes-Benz in Formula One|Mercedes]] drivers [[Kimi Antonelli]] and [[George Russell (racing driver)|George Russell]]. The podium of the main race was completed by Antonelli and [[Red Bull Racing|Red Bull]]'s [[Max Verstappen]], the latter having started from the pit lane.\n\nAfter taking his seventh victory of the season, Norris extended his lead in the [[List of Formula One World Drivers' Champions|Drivers' Championship]] to 24 points over Piastri, with Verstappen a further 25 points adrift in third. While McLaren increased its total to 798 points in the [[List of Formula One World Constructors' Champions|Constructors' Championship]], [[Scuderia Ferrari|Ferrari]] was demoted from second to fourth following their second double retirement of the season, allowing Mercedes and Red Bull to move up to second and third, respectively.\n\n== Background ==\nThe [[São Paulo Grand Prix]] was held at the [[Interlagos Circuit]] in [[São Paulo]] for the 5th time in the circuit's history, having previously held thirty-seven editions of the [[Brazilian Grand Prix]], across the weekend of 7–9 November.<ref>{{cite web |title=Interlagos |url=https://www.statsf1.com/en/circuit-interlagos.aspx |access-date=4 November 2025 |website=Stats F1}}</ref> The Grand Prix was the twenty-first round of the [[2025 Formula One World Championship]] and the fifth held under the name of the [[São Paulo Grand Prix]], having previously been titled the Brazilian Grand Prix.<ref>{{cite web |title=Grands Prix São Paulo |url=https://www.statsf1.com/en/grand-prix-sao-paulo.aspx |access-date=4 November 2025 |website=Stats F1}}</ref> It was also the fifth of six Grands Prix in the season to utilise the [[Formula One racing#Sprint|sprint format]] and the fifth time overall that the São Paulo Grand Prix featured it.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/fia-and-formula-1-announce-2025-sprint-calendar.3fjONI6I9cAyThBj73oQRX|title=FIA and Formula 1 announce 2025 Sprint calendar|website=Formula1.com|date=11 July 2024|access-date=21 July 2025}}</ref>\n\n=== Championship standings before the race ===\nGoing into the weekend, [[Lando Norris]] led the [[List of Formula One World Drivers' Champions|Drivers' Championship]] with 357 points, one point ahead of teammate [[Oscar Piastri]] in second, and 36 points ahead of [[Max Verstappen]]. [[McLaren]], who won the [[List of Formula One World Constructors' Champions|Constructors' Championship]] at the [[2025 Singapore Grand Prix|Singapore Grand Prix]], were ahead of [[Scuderia This tore a tyre off of Leclerc's car. By this point, pit lane starter [[Max Verstappen]] was in thirteenth, but a puncture to the front-left hard tyre forced him to switch to medium tyres. By lap 9 and the conclusion of the virtual safety car period, Piastri was in second place, but would soon receive a ten-second penalty for his role in the Antonelli collision. Similarly, a ten-second penalty for [[Yuki Tsunoda]] followed for causing a collision with Stroll, and another was granted after improperly serving it.<ref name=\":3\" /> \n\nBy lap 56, Verstappen was fourth, behind both Mercedes cars. [[George Russell (racing driver)|George Russell]] had to manage his brake temperature, so he started to lift and coast; Verstappen passed him for third. He encountered Antonelli, who defended second place to mark his highest Formula One career finish at the time. Norris won the race by ten seconds, and Piastri finished in fifth.<ref name=\":3\" />\n\nAfter taking his seventh Grand Prix victory of the season, Norris extended his lead in the Drivers' Championship to 24 points over Piastri, with Verstappen a further 25 points adrift in third. The result also meant that Russell was mathematically eliminated from championship contention, leaving only the top three with chances of winning the Drivers' Championship with three races (including the final sprint in Qatar) to go. While McLaren increased its total to 798 points in the Constructors' Championship, Ferrari was demoted from second to fourth following their second double retirement of the season, allowing Mercedes and Red Bull to move up to second and third, respectively.\n\n=== Race classification ===\n{| class=\"wikitable sortable\" style=\"font-size: 85%;\"\n! scope=\"col\" |{{Tooltip|Pos.|Position}}\n!
