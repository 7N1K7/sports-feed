The [[Australia national cricket team]] is touring South Africa in September and October 2026 to play three [[One Day International]] (ODI) and three [[Test cricket|Test]] matches.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/south-africa-unveil-schedule-for-key-wtc-series |title=South Africa unveil schedule for key WTC series home season |work=[[International Cricket Council]] |access-date=29 March 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cricinfo.com/story/early-season-cape-town-test-headlines-south-africa-s-2026-27-home-season-1525568 |title=Early-season Cape Town Test headlines SA's 2026-27 home season |work=[[Cricinfo]] |access-date=29 March 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cricbuzz.com/cricket-news/137848/south-africa-announce-schedule-for-bumper-mens-home-summer-australia-bangladesh-england-sa-home-summer-sa-vs-aus-sa-vs-eng-sa-vs-ban-2026-cricbuzzcom |title=South Africa announce schedule for bumper men's home summer |work=[[Cricbuzz]] |access-date=29 March 2026}}</ref> The Test series forms part of the [[2025–2027 World Test Championship]] cycle.<ref>{{cite web |title=Men's Future Tours Program |url=https://resources.pulse.icc-cricket.com/ICC/document/2022/08/17/9ecd5af8-4657-475f-ae1e-733b04f69750/Men-s-FTP-upto-2027.pdf |access-date=2 April 2025 |work=[[International Cricket Council]]}}</ref> In February 2026, [[Cricket South Africa]] (CSA) confirmed the fixtures for the tour, as a part of the 2026 home international season.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cricket.co.za/csa-announces-unmissable-2026-27-home-season-for-the-proteas-men/ |title=CSA announces unmissable 2026/27 home season for the Proteas Men |work=[[Cricket South Africa]] |access-date=29 March 2026}}</ref>

The 2026 Test series marked the first time since Australia's [[2018 Australian ball-tampering scandal|controversial]] [[Australian cricket team in South Africa in 2017–18|2017–18 tour]] that Australia had played South Africa in South Africa in Test cricket.<ref>{{cite news |last=Savage |first=Nic |date=24 February 2026 |title=Dates for Australia’s first Test tour of South Africa since Cape Town scandal revealed |url=https://www.foxsports.com.au/cricket/dates-for-australias-first-test-tour-of-south-africa-since-cape-town-scandal-revealed/news-story/b64888b88699669eb80be9a872f2679b |work=[[Fox Sports (Australia)|Fox Sports Australia]] |publisher=[[News Corp Australia]] }}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last=Pollock |first=Shaun |date=8 October 2026 |title=South Africa vs Australia Test series: 'Sandpapergate' adds to intrigue in rematch of infamous 2018 ball-fixing scandal |url=https://www.skysports.com/cricket/news/13595454/south-africa-vs-australia-test-series-sandpapergate-adds-to-intrigue-in-rematch-of-infamous-2018-ball-fixing-scandal |work=[[Sky Sports]] }}</ref>

On 12 September, [[Ottneil Baartman]] was ruled out from the ODI series due to a hamstring injury and was replaced by [[Duan Jansen]].<ref>{{cite web |first=Firdose |last=Moonda |date=13 September 2026 |url=https://www.cricinfo.com/story/south-africa-news-ottneil-baartman-out-of-australia-odis-duan-jansen-named-replacement-1553992 |title=Injured Baartman out of Australia ODIs, Duan Jansen named replacement |work=[[Cricinfo]] |access-date=19 September 2026}}</ref> On 18 September, [[Lungi Ngidi]] was ruled out from the ODI series due to a hamstring injury and was replaced by [[Nqobani Mokoena]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/injury-rules-out-star-south-african-pacer-for-australia-series |title=Injury rules out star South African pacer for Australia series |work=[[International Cricket Council]] |date=18 September 2026 |access-date=24 September 2026}}</ref> On 23 September, [[Kwena Maphaka]] was ruled out from the ODI series due to a right hamstring strain and was replaced by [[Lizaad Williams]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.cricinfo.com/story/sa-vs-aus-odis-lizaad-williams-replaces-kwena-maphaka-as-south-africa-s-hamstring-crisis-intensifies-1555463 |title=Williams replaces Maphaka as SA's hamstring crisis intensifies |work=[[Cricinfo]] |date=23 September 2026 |access-date=28 September 2026}}</ref>

On 21 September, [[Billy Stanlake]] and [[Spencer Johnson (cricketer)|Spencer Johnson]] were ruled out of the ODI series following a side strain and management, [[Jack Edwards (cricketer, born 2000)|Jack Edwards]] was named in their place.<ref>{{cite web |last=Cameron |first=Louis |date=21 September 2026 |title=Edwards added to Aussie squad, pace duo head home |url=https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4579271/australia-odi-squad-selection-changes-jack-edwards-spencer-johnson-billy-stanlake-south-africa-tour? |work=[[Cricket Australia]] |location=[[Harare]] |access-date=27 September 2026}}</ref> On 29 September, both [[Nathan Ellis]] and [[Joel Davies]] were ruled out of the last ODI due to side strain and a hamstring strain, respectively with no replacememnts named.<ref>{{cite web |last=Malcolm |first=Alex |date=29 September 2026 |title=Ellis and Davies out of the South Africa tour with injury |url=https://www.cricinfo.com/story/nathan-ellis-and-joel-davies-out-of-the-south-africa-tour-with-injury-1556070 |work=[[Cricinfo]] |access-date=30 September 2026}}</ref> On 1 October, [[Josh Inglis]] was ruled out of the Test series due to fractured finger and was replaced by [[Sam Harper (cricketer)|Sam Harper]]. [[Cameron Green]]'s availability was uncertain after he sustained lower abdominal strain, [[Nic Maddinson]] was added in the squad for his cover.<ref>{{cite web |access-date=1 October 2026 |title=Australia make squad changes ahead of first Proteas Test |url=https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/australia-make-squad-changes-ahead-of-first-proteas-test |work=International Cricket Council}}</ref> Later, Inglis rejoined the squad for remaining matches.<ref>{{cite web |access-date=10 October 2026 |title=Inglis to rejoin Australia Test squad after finger injury |url=https://www.cricinfo.com/story/josh-inglis-to-rejoin-australia-test-squad-after-finger-injury-1557845?platform=amp |website=Cricinfo}}</ref> 

On 15 October, [[Matthew Breetzke]] was added to South Africa's squad as a replacement for [[Tristan Stubbs]] who was dropped from the squad.<ref>{{cite web |access-date=15 October 2026 |title=Stubbs dropped, Rickelton to keep wickets in Gqeberha Test |url=https://www.cricinfo.com/story/sa-vs-aus-2nd-test-tristan-stubbs-dropped-ryan-rickelton-to-keep-wickets-1558532 |work=[[Cricinfo]]}}</ref>

==Venues==
In February 2026, [[Cricket South Africa]] (CSA) announced their men's team home schedule, confirming the ODI and Test venues for the series against Australia. [[Durban]]'s [[Kingsmead Cricket Ground]], [[Johannesburg]]'s [[Wanderers Stadium]], [[Potchefstroom]]'s [[JB Marks Oval]], [[Gqeberha]]'s [[St George's Park Cricket Ground]], and [[Cape Town]]'s [[Newlands Cricket Ground]] were revealed to host the three ODIs and three Test matches, respectively.<ref>{{cite web |date=23 February 2026 |title=CSA Announces Unmissable 2026/27 Home Season for The Proteas Men |url=https://www.cricket.co.za/the-proteas-men-2026-27-home-fixtures-csa/ |work=cricket.co.za |publisher=[[Cricket South Africa]] }}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last=Kaushik |first=Aditya |date=24 September 2026 |title=Australia tour of South Africa 2026: Full schedule, venues, live streaming |url=https://www.business-standard.com/cricket/news/australia-tour-of-south-africa-2026-full-schedule-venues-live-streaming-126091500769_1.html |work=[[Business Standard]] }}</ref>
