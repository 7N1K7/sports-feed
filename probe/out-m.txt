'''Pool A of the 2026 Rugby League World Cup''' is one of the three groups in the [[2026 Men's Rugby League World Cup|2026 Rugby League World Cup]]. The group comprises hosts [[Australia national rugby league team|Australia]] and [[New Zealand national rugby league team|New Zealand]], as well as automatic qualifiers [[Fiji national rugby league team|Fiji]], and [[Cook Islands national rugby league team|Cook Islands]], who qualified through the [[2026 Men's Rugby League World Cup qualification#Inter-confederation play-offs|Southern Hemisphere play-offs]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nrl.com/news/2026/10/16/only-one-jumper-for-me-iro-cooks-up-something-special/|title=Only one jumper for me: Iro cooks up something special|date=16 October 2026|website=NRL.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nrl.com/news/2025/11/09/cook-islands-claim-last-world-cup-berth/|title=Cook Islands claim last World Cup berth|date=9 November 2025|website=NRL.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.foxsports.com.au/nrl/world-cup/rugby-league-world-cup-2026-schedule-draw-fixtures-pools-dates-and-times-final-how-many-teams-go-through-from-each-pool/news-story/6cc5826befd16b9fee93091b2198dbf4|title=Full 2026 Rugby League World Cup schedule... and how it works|date=13 October 2026|website=Fox Sports}}</ref>
