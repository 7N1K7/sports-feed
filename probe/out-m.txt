{{Short description|Rugby League World Cup results}}
'''Pools B and C of the 2026 Rugby League World Cup''' are two of the three pools in the [[2026 Men's Rugby League World Cup|2026 Rugby League World Cup]]. Pool B comprises automatic qualifiers [[England national rugby league team|England]], [[Lebanon national rugby league team|Lebanon]] and [[Samoa national rugby league team|Samoa]]. Pool C comprises hosts [[Papua New Guinea national rugby league team|Papua New Guinea]], automatic qualifiers [[Tonga national rugby league team|Tonga]], and [[France national rugby league team|France]], who qualified through the [[2026 Men's Rugby League World Cup qualification#Inter-confederation play-offs|Northern Hemisphere play-offs]].

Rather than playing the other two teams in their pool, each nation plays the three teams from the other pool.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nrl.com/news/2026/10/16/must-win-unique-world-cup-structure-raises-the-stakes/|title=Must-win: Unique World Cup structure raises the stakes|date=16 October 2026|website=NRL.com}}</ref>
