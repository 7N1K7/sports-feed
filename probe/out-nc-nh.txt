The '''2026 Nations Championship Northern Hemisphere Series''' will be the second phase of matches (rounds 4–6) in the inaugural edition of the [[Nations Championship]], a biennial international men's [[rugby union]] competition between the [[Six Nations Championship|Six Nations]] sides, [[SANZAAR]], [[Fiji national rugby union team|Fiji]] and [[Japan national rugby union team|Japan]].<ref name="SixNations">{{cite web |title=Hemispheres collide and rugby's future redefined with 2026 Nations Championship |url=https://www.sixnationsrugby.com/en/autumn-nations-series/news/nations-champions-launch-2026 |website=Six Nations Rugby |access-date=19 November 2025}}</ref><ref name="BBC">{{Cite web |last=Henson |first=Mike |date=17 November 2025 |title=Nations Championship to feature north v south hemisphere contest |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/rugby-union/articles/c0rpl9v1ly5o |access-date=17 November 2025 |website=BBC Sport}}</ref>

The Northern Hemisphere Series will take place between 6 November and 21 November, during the [[End-of-year rugby union internationals|November International window]]. The matches replaces the traditional tours between the travelling SANZAAR, the leading Tier 2 nations in the Pacific Islands and Japan, and the Six Nation sides.
