The '''2026 World Rugby Nations Cup Americas-Pacific Series''' was the first phase of matches (rounds 1–3) of the inaugural edition of the [[World Rugby Nations Cup (2026–)|World Rugby Nations Cup]], the second tier of a biennial international men's [[rugby union]] competition, predominantly made of Tier 2 nations from [[Rugby Africa|Africa]], [[Rugby Americas North|Americas North]], [[Asia Rugby|Asia]], [[Rugby Europe|Europe]] and [[Sudamérica Rugby|Sudamérica]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=17 November 2025 |title=Canada, Chile, Uruguay and USA all in 2026 World Rugby Nations Cup |url=https://www.americasrugbynews.com/2025/11/17/canada-chile-uruguay-and-usa-all-in-2026-world-rugby-nations-cup/ |access-date=20 November 2025 |website=Americas Rugby News}}</ref><ref name="WR-2">{{cite web|url=https://www.world.rugby/news/1043084/world-rugby-nations-cup-heads-to-the-americas-in-july-for-inaugural-2026-edition|title=World Rugby Nations Cup heads to the Americas in July for inaugural 2026 edition|website=World Rugby|date=31 March 2026|access-date=31 March 2026}}</ref>

The Americas-Pacific Series took place between 4 July and 18 July, during the [[Mid-year rugby union internationals|July International window]]. The matches replaced the traditional tour matches between the traditional travelling European sides to the various Tier 2 nations around the world.

==Fixtures==
Matches were held in the Americas region, with [[Samoa national rugby union team|Samoa]] playing their matches in South America and [[Tonga national rugby union team|Tonga]] playing their matches in North America. Each of the European visiting teams played all their matches in either North America or all in South America to reduce travel.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.world.rugby/news/1018591?lang=en|title=World Rugby Nations Cup to launch in July 2026 to transform international calendar &#124; World Rugby|website=World Rugby}}</ref> Fixtures and venues were announced by World Rugby on 31 March 2026.<ref name="WR-2" />
