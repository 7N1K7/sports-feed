The '''2026 Wheelchair Rugby League World Cup''' will be the fifth staging of the [[Wheelchair Rugby League World Cup]], and will be one of three major tournaments part of the [[2026 Rugby League World Cup]].

The competition was to be held in during October and  November 2025,<ref>{{cite web |title=2025 Rugby League World Cup: France promises low carbon event after being named host |url=https://www.sportspromedia.com/news/rugby-league-world-cup-2025-france-host-sustainability/ |website=SportsPro |access-date=25 March 2022 |date=11 January 2022}}</ref> but was moved to 2026 following the withdrawal of France as hosts nation.<ref name="26Confirmed1">{{Cite web |date=2023-08-03 |title=Southern hemisphere to host 2026 World Cup |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/rugby-league/66390816 |access-date=2023-08-10 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref><ref name="26Confirmed2">{{Cite web |title=Reduced Rugby League World Cup to take place in 2026|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/aug/03/rugby-league-world-cup-announced-2026 |access-date=2023-08-10 |website=The Guardian|date=3 August 2023 }}</ref><ref name="26Confirmed3">{{Cite web |date=2023-08-03 |title=2026 Rugby League World Cup to be hosted in southern hemisphere |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/australia-france-new-zealand-b2386872.html |access-date=2023-08-10 |website=The Independent |language=en}}</ref><ref name="26Confirmed4">{{Cite web |date=2023-08-03 |title=Rugby League World Cup 2025 to be delayed; full details announced for new look tournament |url=https://www.loverugbyleague.com/post/rugby-league-world-cup-2025-to-be-delayed-full-details-announced-for-new-look-tournament |access-date=2023-08-10 |website=LoveRugbyLeague |language=en}}</ref> The competition will run in parallel with the men's and women's tournaments.<ref>{{cite news |title=France 2025: 13 questions answered about the Rugby League World Cup |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/no-helmets-required/2022/jan/19/france-2025-rugby-league-world-cup-england |access-date=25 March 2022 |work=[[The Guardian]] |date=19 January 2022}}</ref>

Due to the rescheduling, the competition will feature 8 teams only. An expansion was planned to double the number of teams for the original 2025 tournament.<ref name="26Confirmed1" /><ref name="26Confirmed2" /><ref name="26Confirmed3" /><ref name="26Confirmed4" />

Ahead of the tournament, [[Australia national wheelchair rugby league team|Australia]] and [[New Zealand national wheelchair rugby league team|New Zealand]] were reclassified as Tier 2 nations for the wheelchair game by the [[International Rugby League]] in recognition of [[England national wheelchair rugby league team|England]]'s and [[France national wheelchair rugby league team|France]]'s domination of the sport since its inception.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.intrl.sport/article/724/changes-to-wheelchair-rugby-league-eligibility-tiers |title=Changes to wheelchair rugby league eligibility tiers |website=International Rugby League |date=20 May 2026 |access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref>

==Host selection==
{{see also|2025 Rugby League World Cup#Host selection}}
The [[International Rugby League]] (IRL) originally decided in 2016 to have the tournaments hosted in the United States and Canada.<ref name="America1">{{cite news 
  | title = Rugby League World Cup moves to North America in 2025
  | publisher = stuff sport
  | date = 20 November 2016
| url = http://www.stuff.co.nz/sport/league/86672646/rugby-league-world-cup-moves-to-north-america-in-2025
  | accessdate = 21 November 2016}}</ref><ref name="America2">{{Cite web |date=2016-12-01 |title=North America to Host 2025 Rugby league World Cup |url=https://www.canadarugbyleague.com/north-america-host-2025-rugby-league-world-cup/ |access-date=2023-05-17 |website=Canada Rugby League Association |language=en-CA}}</ref> In December 2019 however, the IRL withdrew the hosting rights due to the promoters, Moore Sports International, being unable to guarantee the staging of the tournaments.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.smh.com.au/sport/nrl/us-world-cup-hosting-plans-torpedoed-by-money-trouble-20181204-p50k5p.html |title=US World Cup hosting plans torpedoed by money trouble |newspaper=Sydney Morning Herald |author= Adrian Proszenko |date=4 December 2018 |access-date= 25 March 2022}}</ref><ref name="torpedoed">{{cite news|url=https://www.smh.com.au/sport/nrl/us-world-cup-hosting-plans-torpedoed-by-money-trouble-20181204-p50k5p.html |title=US World Cup hosting plans torpedoed by money trouble |newspaper=Sydney Morning Herald |author= Adrian Proszenko |date=4 December 2018 |access-date= 28 December 2018}}</ref>

After re-opening the bidding to host the tournaments, the IRL awarded the hosting rights to France.<ref name="France1">{{Cite web|date=8 January 2022|title=France to host 2025 World Cup|url=https://www.nrl.com/news/2022/01/09/france-to-host-2025-world-cup/|access-date=8 January 2022|website=National Rugby League}}</ref><ref name="France3">{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2022-01-11 |title=France set to host the 2025 Rugby League World Cups |url=https://asiapacificrl.com/2022/01/11/france-set-to-host-the-2025-rugby-league-world-cups/ |access-date=2023-05-17 |website=Asia Pacific Rugby League |language=en-US |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220127060604/https://asiapacificrl.com/2022/01/11/france-set-to-host-the-2025-rugby-league-world-cups/ |archive-date=2022-01-27}}</ref> On 15 May 2023, the France 2025 organising committee was forced to withdraw from hosting the tournament, due to financial concerns from the [[Borne government|new French government]] elected in May 2022.<ref name="Withdraw1">{{cite news |title=France pulls out of hosting Rugby League World Cup |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/rugby-league/65601619 |access-date=15 May 2023 |work=BBC Sport |date=15 May 2023}}</ref><ref name="Withdraw3">{{cite news |last1=Bower |first1=Aaron |title=2025 Rugby League World Cup in doubt after France pull out of staging event |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/may/15/2025-rugby-league-world-cup-in-doubt-after-france-pull-out-of-staging-event |access-date=15 May 2023 |work=[[The Guardian]] |date=15 May 2023}}</ref>

On 3 August 2023 it was confirmed that the tournament would be moved to 2026 and held in the Southern Hemisphere.<ref name="26Confirmed1" /><ref name="26Confirmed2" /> On 24 July 2024, the IRL announced that [[Australia]] would host the 2026 tournament.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-07-24 |title=Australia to host 2026 World Cup |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/rugby-league/articles/czd92pqdj81o |access-date=2024-07-24 |website=BBC Sport |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nrl.com/news/2024/07/24/arlc-welcomes-rugby-league-world-cup-2026-hosting-rights/|title=ARLC welcomes Rugby League World Cup 2026 hosting rights|date=24 July 2024|website=National Rugby League}}</ref>

===Qualification===
On 3 August 2023, the date the tournament was confirmed, the only qualified teams for the 2026 tournament were the semi-finalists of the [[2021 Wheelchair Rugby League World Cup|2021 World Cup]].

*{{wrl-l|AUS}} 
*{{wrl-l|ENG}} 
*{{wrl-l|FRA}} 
*{{wrl-l|WAL}} 

On 15 April 2025, the [[International Rugby League]] announced two further participants as representatives of The Americas and [[Asia-Pacific Rugby League]] respectively:<ref name="IRLmay" />

*{{wrl-l|USA}} 
*{{wrl-l|NZL}} 

The final four wheelchair rugby league nations, all being [[European Rugby League]] representatives, would have to submit applications for the final two places.<ref name="IRLmay">{{cite web|url=https://www.intrl.sport/article/417/new-zealand,-usa-confirm-wheelchair-berths-at-rlwc2026 |title=New Zealand, USA confirm Wheelchair berths at RLWC2026 |website=International Rugby League |date=15 April 2025 |access-date=13 May 2025}}</ref>

On 10 September 2025, the two successful European applicants were announced as:<ref name="IRLseptember" />

*{{wrl-l|IRE}}
*{{wrl-l|SCO}}

The application process assessed teams based on senior international matches played since the 2021 World Cup, preparation for the 2026 World Cup, domestic competition strength, and participation impact for the 2026 World Cup.<ref name="IRLseptember">{{Cite web|url=https://www.intrl.sport/article/504/ireland,-scotland-secure-remaining-irl-wheelchair-rlwc26-berths|title=Ireland, Scotland secure remaining IRL Wheelchair RLWC26 berths|website=Rugby League International Federation|accessdate=24 November 2025}}</ref>
