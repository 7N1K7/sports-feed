The '''2026 Women's Rugby League World Cup''' is the seventh staging of the [[Women's Rugby League World Cup]], and is one of three major tournaments that make up the [[2026 Rugby League World Cup]].

The competition was to be held in during October and  November 2025,<ref>{{cite web |title=2025 Rugby League World Cup: France promises low carbon event after being named host |url=https://www.sportspromedia.com/news/rugby-league-world-cup-2025-france-host-sustainability/ |website=SportsPro |access-date=25 March 2022 |date=11 January 2022}}</ref> but was moved to 2026 following the withdrawal of France as hosts nation.<ref name="26Confirmed1">{{Cite web |date=2023-08-03 |title=Southern hemisphere to host 2026 World Cup |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/rugby-league/66390816 |access-date=2023-08-10 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref><ref name="26Confirmed2">{{Cite web |title=Reduced Rugby League World Cup to take place in 2026 |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2023/aug/03/rugby-league-world-cup-announced-2026 |access-date=2023-08-10 |website=The Guardian|date=3 August 2023 }}</ref><ref name="26Confirmed3">{{Cite web |date=2023-08-03 |title=2026 Rugby League World Cup to be hosted in southern hemisphere |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/australia-france-new-zealand-b2386872.html |access-date=2023-08-10 |website=The Independent |language=en}}</ref><ref name="26Confirmed4">{{Cite web |date=2023-08-03 |title=Rugby League World Cup 2025 to be delayed; full details announced for new look tournament |url=https://www.loverugbyleague.com/post/rugby-league-world-cup-2025-to-be-delayed-full-details-announced-for-new-look-tournament |access-date=2023-08-10 |website=LoveRugbyLeague |language=en}}</ref> The competition runs in parallel with the men's and wheelchair tournaments.<ref>{{cite news |title=France 2025: 13 questions answered about the Rugby League World Cup |url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/no-helmets-required/2022/jan/19/france-2025-rugby-league-world-cup-england |access-date=25 March 2022 |work=[[The Guardian]] |date=19 January 2022}}</ref>

Due to the rescheduling, the competition features eight teams only. An expansion was planned to double the number of teams for the original 2025 tournament.<ref name="26Confirmed1" /><ref name="26Confirmed2" /><ref name="26Confirmed3" /><ref name="26Confirmed4" />

==Teams==
===Qualification===
{{main|2026 Women's Rugby League World Cup qualification}}

On 3 August 2023, the date the tournament was announced, the only qualified teams for the 2026 tournament were the semi-finalists of the [[2021 Women's Rugby League World Cup|2021 World Cup]]. The qualification process for the remaining teams was announced 15 December 2023.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.intrl.sport/news/rlwc2026-women-s-qualifying-process-announced/|title=Rugby League World Cup 2026 women's qualifying process announced|website=Rugby League International Federation|date=15 December 2023|access-date=11 January 2024}}</ref>

Two teams would qualify directly via the European qualifications group stages on 22 June 2024,<ref>{{cite web |title=France, Wales women qualify for RLWC2026 |url=https://www.intrl.sport/article/179/france,-wales-women-qualify-for-rlwc2026 |website=Rugby League International Federation |access-date=24 June 2024 |date=23 June 2024}}</ref> and one team would qualify directly via the [[2024 Rugby League Pacific Championships#Women's Pacific Bowl|2024 Women's Pacific Bowl]] on 2 November 2024.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nrl.com/news/2024/11/02/late-surge-earns-fetu-samoa-world-cup-qualification-and-promotion-shot/ |title=Late surge earns Samoa World Cup spot, promotion shot |website=National Rugby League |date=2 November 2024 |access-date=2 November 2024}}</ref>

The [[2026 Women's Rugby League World Cup qualification #World Series|2025 World Series]] determined the final team to qualify, with one team from each confederation qualifying for the World Series.<ref>{{cite web |title=Match Detail |url=https://www.internationalrugbyleague.com/match-details/2517 |website=Rugby League International Federation |access-date=24 November 2025}}</ref>

== Rule changes ==
Contrary to the international laws of the game,<ref>https://www.intrl.sport/about/laws-of-the-game</ref> matches of the 2026 women's world cup will be 70 minutes long, matching Australia's domestic competition – the [[NRL Women's Premiership|NRLW]]. This was done to "ensure consistency and familiarity for fans, broadcasters and key tournament stakeholders, as well as coaches and players".<ref>https://www.intrl.sport/article/711/irl-rugby-league-world-cup-2026-rules-confirmed-</ref>
