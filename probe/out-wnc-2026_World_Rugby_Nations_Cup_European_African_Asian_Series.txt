The '''2026 World Rugby Nations Cup European-African-Asian Series''' will be the second phase of matches (rounds 4–6) in the inaugural edition of the [[World Rugby Nations Cup (2026–)|World Rugby Nations Cup]], the second tier of a biennial international men's [[rugby union]] competition, predominantly made of Tier 2 nations from [[Rugby Africa|Africa]], [[Rugby Americas North|Americas North]], [[Asia Rugby|Asia]], [[Rugby Europe|Europe]] and [[Sudamérica Rugby|Sudamérica]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=17 November 2025 |title=Canada, Chile, Uruguay and USA all in 2026 World Rugby Nations Cup |url=https://www.americasrugbynews.com/2025/11/17/canada-chile-uruguay-and-usa-all-in-2026-world-rugby-nations-cup/ |access-date=20 November 2025 |website=Americas Rugby News}}</ref><ref name="WR-2">{{cite web|url=https://www.world.rugby/news/1043084/world-rugby-nations-cup-heads-to-the-americas-in-july-for-inaugural-2026-edition|title=World Rugby Nations Cup heads to the Americas in July for inaugural 2026 edition|website=World Rugby|date=31 March 2026|access-date=31 March 2026}}</ref>

The European-African-Asian Series will take place between 6 November and 21 November, during the [[End-of-year rugby union internationals|November International window]]. The matches replaces the traditional tours between the travelling Tier 2 nations from the Pacific Islands and Americas to Europe.

==Fixtures==
European teams will host all of their matches at home. [[Zimbabwe national rugby union team|Zimbabwe]] will host their games in England. [[Hong Kong national rugby union team|Hong Kong]] will play their first two matches in Europe, with the third (against Tonga) being held in Hong Kong.<ref>[https://www.scmp.com/sport/rugby/hong-kong-rugby-union/article/3350611/world-rugby-boss-robinson-hails-hong-kongs-global-influence-wxv-announcement?module=perpetual_scroll_0&pgtype=article World Rugby boss Robinson hails Hong Kong's ‘global influence’ at WXV announcement]</ref> Confirmation of venues will come in due course for the second leg of matches played in November.<ref>[https://www.world.rugby/nations-cup/en/news/1043086/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-world-rugby-nations-cup Everything you need to know about the World Rugby Nations Cup]</ref>
