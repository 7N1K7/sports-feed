                    Not counted: cloud servers

                    Stopped at the daily limit

                    From 25 Oct 2026. A network is the internet provider a visitor outside NZ connects through: a phone or home provider is a person. A page run from a cloud company's servers (Amazon, Google, Microsoft and the like) is a script, a link checker or a search engine, so those are left out of every other number here, and so is a VPN on those networks. From 13 Nov 2026, one address that sends more than 2,000 messages in a day is a script posing as many visitors: it's stopped there for the rest of the day and listed once under the daily limit (an NZ address just as NZ).

                    Speed and errors

                    Errors on visitors' devices

                    Problems in the site's own code; each is worth a look. ✓ Fixed marks one already dealt with (counts from before the fix stay in the range); ⚠ Back means a page with the fix sent it again. From v404 each error ends with the version of the page that sent it, [v404].

                    Site checks

                    The live site, checked from outside every 10 minutes (from 3 Nov 2026): the page and its feed answer, and the feed is whole and less than 90 minutes old. A problem's times are the checks either side of it, so it's known to 10 minutes. A problem only one visitor's connection had doesn't show here: a page that had to ask for its feed again shows in Errors as "Feed asked again". Kept 30 days.

                    Feed build

                    What went wrong in the feed's last build, from the public feed's own notes: a game id used twice, news or Wikipedia pages that couldn't be read, TheSportsDB errors, and hand-kept games their source moved.
